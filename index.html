Destination Customs & Duties (DDU)

Every international order is dispatched under Delivered Duty Unpaid (DDU) terms. Any local customs duties, import tariffs, VAT, or clearance fees determined by the destination country's customs authority (including the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, and others) are the sole responsibility of the buyer and must be settled upon arrival.

Return & Reshipment Policy

If an order returns to Brazil due to unpaid customs duties, incorrect delivery address, or buyer refusal, return shipping fees and re-dispatch costs will be billed to the buyer prior to any reshipment.
